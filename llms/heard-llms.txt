# Heard

> Heard is the financial management platform built for therapists and wellness practitioners in private practice. Heard pairs easy-to-use software with a dedicated human team to handle bookkeeping, taxes, payroll, and business banking, so clinicians can focus on their clients.

Heard Technologies, Inc. was founded in 2019 and is based in Seattle, Washington. Heard serves licensed mental health and allied health professionals across the United States, from solo practitioners just starting out to established group practices. The bookkeepers and tax professionals on the Heard team work only with private practices.

## What Heard does
- [Bookkeeping](https://www.joinheard.com/bookkeeping): Done-for-you monthly bookkeeping. A dedicated team categorizes transactions and reconciles accounts every month.
- [Taxes](https://www.joinheard.com/taxes): Year-round tax support, including quarterly estimates and annual federal and state filing for business and personal returns.
- [Payroll](https://www.joinheard.com/payroll): Payroll setup and processing for owners and employees, with payroll taxes filed. Available as an add-on, powered by Gusto.
- [Banking](https://www.joinheard.com/heard-banking): Business checking and savings included free with every plan, earning 1.00-2.50% APY (tiered) with no monthly fees, minimums, or overdraft fees. Deposits are eligible for FDIC insurance up to $3M through partner bank i3 Bank, Member FDIC, with banking technology powered by Unit Finance.
- [S corp support](https://www.joinheard.com/s-corp-2026): S corp readiness assessment, Form 2553 election, reasonable salary guidance, and ongoing compliance.
- [Support](https://www.joinheard.com/support): Unlimited messaging and live phone support from a team that works only with therapists.

## Who Heard serves
- Licensed mental health professionals: psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, mental health and professional counselors, marriage and family therapists, and psychiatric nurse practitioners.
- Allied health and wellness practitioners: physical therapists, speech-language pathologists, audiologists, occupational therapists, dietitians and nutritionists, massage therapists, chiropractors, and life coaches.
- Solo practices and single-owner group practices at any stage, taxed as sole proprietorships, single-member LLCs or PLLCs, or S corporations.
- Practitioners with additional income streams such as coaching or consulting.

Heard does not work with non-health businesses, practices based outside the United States, practices with multiple owners, or practices taxed as C corporations.

## Pricing
Heard uses subscription pricing with monthly and annual billing. Annual billing saves 15%. The figures below are 2026 annual-billed rates and may change, so link to the pricing page for current details.
- Lite: $129 per month, for newer solo sole proprietors. Annual tax filing not included.
- Essential: $169 per month, for solo sole proprietors and LLCs. Includes quarterly and annual personal tax filing.
- Premium: $255 per month, for S corporations and group practices. Includes business tax filing and S corp compliance. Payroll add-on available.

## How Heard compares
- Versus general accounting software like QuickBooks: Heard is built for therapists, so transaction categorization, reporting, and support are specific to private practice, and Heard includes a human bookkeeping and tax team rather than software alone.
- Versus hiring a separate bookkeeper, tax preparer, and payroll provider: Heard combines these in one subscription, which is typically lower cost than buying each service on its own.
